How to play

🚪 Tap a courtroom. Each one is a topic from Prof. Segall’s course, weighted toward separation of powers and federalism. 200 exam-style questions in all: multiple choice and select-all-that-apply.

🔀 Fresh docket every visit. Each courtroom deals a short round from a bigger pool. Cases you haven’t seen come first, and the questions and answer choices are reshuffled every time.

🔓 Right answer = the cell opens and your client walks free. Wrong answer = the bars slam shut, you lose a life, and the client goes to your Missed Clients file to retry later.

❤️ 3 lives per courtroom. Streaks raise your multiplier (×1 → ×1.5 → ×2 → ×2.5 → ×3).

🤝 Ask a Colleague removes wrong choices (50/50). ☕ Coffee Chat gives +1 life (or a shield if you’re full). One of each per courtroom.

🚨 Code Blue adds a 30-second clock (35s for select-all) and bonus points. The gavel speeds up when things get tense.

👑 Courtroom 12 is the boss fight: right answers push the boss client toward freedom; wrong answers drag him back to jail.

Every answer comes with a plain-English “why.” Questions come from the class notes and Week 14 exam guidance. The real exam has 1–5 right answers per question, so select-all items get extra reps.
